About

Science Club
A place to discuss SCIENCE.

Do Science! is an institute-independent initiative started by PhD students and postdocs at IIMCB and Biocentrum Ochota. We invite excellent scientists, from Warsaw and from abroad, to give a lecture and then stay for an informal conversation.

The mix is deliberate: Nobel laureates next to young Warsaw group leaders, ribosomes next to bats, neuroscience next to genomics. The common thread is curiosity and a room full of early-career researchers who ask questions.

More than lectures

SciEvents, trips, mini-courses

Besides the lecture series we keep SciEvents, a shared calendar of bioscience events in Warsaw that anyone can add to. Over the years the club has also run journal clubs, an RNA mini-symposium, a two-week mini-course and an outing to an exhibition.

RNA Club Warsaw started in 2016 as a spin-off of Do Science! and restarts in October 2026 as a monthly series on the Ochota Campus.

29 September 2026

We are relaunching Do Science! in Warsaw, and we need organisers

Do Science! is a simple idea. PhD students and postdocs invite a scientist they want to hear, and the speaker gives a lecture. Afterwards comes the part we like best: a career advice talk and a beer, without the PIs.

Since 2013, when we started Do Science! with Wojciech Siwek and many others, we have hosted three Nobel laureates and around forty other speakers (see the full list of meetings). The next round should be picked by the people who will sit in the audience.

Do Science! and RNA Club Warsaw can live side by side on the Ochota Campus. RNA Club brings the labs together. Do Science! is for early-career researchers who want to meet the people whose work they read.

If you are a PhD student or postdoc at IIMCB, CeNT, IBB, Nencki, or anywhere else in Warsaw, join the team. You choose the speakers. We will help with invitations and logistics, and stay out of the way. Shoot us a message.

Meeting format

How a Do Science! evening works

  1. 18:00
    Lecture and discussionThe speaker's science, for a broad life-science audience. Students and postdocs ask first.
  2. 19:15
    Career adviceHow the speaker got where they are, and what they would do differently.
  3. ~19:45
    Coffee or beer with the speakerAt a place near the campus, such as Mam Ochotę or Jeleń na Rykowisku.
